## What the system is actually doing (measured)

- **GPU:** the Samsung Odyssey G81SF (QD-OLED, 4K 240 Hz) is on `card0-HDMI-A-1`, the **Radeon 610M
  iGPU** (2 CUs, Granite Ridge). The RTX 5090 (`card1`) drives nothing. CPU 9800X3D, 186 GB RAM.
- **Refresh rate:** the monitor runs at **3840x2160 @ 60 Hz, 8-bit (XRGB8888)**. `monitors.lua`
  (generated by nwg-displays at 22:13, before the 22:29 reboot) configures `HDMI-A-4` @ 240 Hz 10-bit
  and mirrors a 1280x720 `HDMI-A-3` onto it. Neither name exists this boot, so no rule matches and
  Hyprland fell back to the preferred mode. The iGPU's HDMI port offers at most 4K @ 120 Hz; 240 Hz
  only exists on the 5090.
- **Surfaces:** 16 layer surfaces are mapped, two of them full 4K: `qs-shell` (Overlay, blurred) and
  `qs-shadow` (Top). The four regions (`qs-region-*`, Overlay, three of them blurred) and their four
  shadow surfaces cover about 1.5 more screens. Plus `qs-bar`, `qs-media` and four `qs-frame-space`.
- **Memory:** `qs` RSS 674 MB.
- **IPC cost:** `qs ipc call` takes 13-20 ms per call (plus Hyprland's `sh -c`); `hyprctl` takes 2 ms.
- **hypridle is not installed** (`pacman -Q hypridle`: not found); `hl.exec_cmd("hypridle")` fails silently.
- **Legacy dispatch syntax is rejected** by the Lua config (tested with a dispatch to a nonexistent
  window: `')' expected near 'address'`), so every `hyprctl dispatch <name> <args>` does nothing.
- **Quickshell crashed** at 22:11 (SIGSEGV in `QWindow::setScreen`, while the monitors were being
  reconfigured) and nothing restarted it.
- **hyprshot is not installed**, so the Print, Alt+Print and Ctrl+Print binds do nothing.
- Installed and relevant: Hyprland 0.56.2, Quickshell 0.3.2, Qt 6.12 (has `RectangularShadow`),
  qt6-shadertools (`qsb`), Quickshell's `ScriptModel`, `HyprlandFocusGrab`, `GlobalShortcut`,
  `Hyprland.dispatch`, `PwNodeLinkTracker`; Hyprland's `hl.dsp.global`, `render.new_render_scheduling`,
  `render.direct_scanout`, `cursor.min_refresh_rate` (see `/usr/share/hypr/stubs/hl.meta.lua`).
- 90 wallpapers in `~/wallpapers/walls` (86 jpg, 3 JPG, 1 png). `state.json` is 57 KB.

Cost estimate (not measured yet, see Testing): on the 610M one full-screen 4K blended pass is roughly
1-2 ms, and anything that damages the whole screen (a workspace slide, every frame of a live wallpaper,
a video) makes Hyprland blend roughly 6-8 screens' worth of shell surfaces. The frame budget is 16.7 ms
at 60 Hz and 8.3 ms at 120 Hz.

## 1. High-level design

**1.1 Which GPU drives the display** (decision for Shubham; everything else depends on it).
- Cable on the 5090 (DP 2.1 / HDMI 2.1): 240 Hz 10-bit, and blur, shadows and 4K surfaces stop
  mattering; most of this list becomes optional. `hyprland.lua` already sets the NVIDIA environment
  for that case. Cost: some VRAM, and the desktop shares the GPU with compute jobs.
- Stay on the iGPU (reasonable if the 5090 is kept free for LLMs/compute): 120 Hz at most, and 1.2-1.6
  are what make 120 Hz hold.

**1.2 Shrink what stays mapped.** For any damage Hyprland blends every surface overlapping it,
transparent pixels included, and runs blur passes for the blurred ones. `qs-shell` is full-screen only
so a click anywhere closes a panel (`quickshell/shell.qml:113-131`).
- Use `HyprlandFocusGrab { active: win.anyOpen; windows: [bar, dock, region surfaces]; onCleared:
  win.closeOthers("") }`. It should also hand the panel the keyboard, replacing the 200 ms
  exclusive-grab workaround (`quickshell/FrameRegion.qml:66-79`).
- Then `qs-shell` and `qs-shadow` become thin edge surfaces (bands + 28 px corners + shadow fade),
  about 6% of the screen instead of two full screens. The dock buttons go on the left band's surface.
- Optional end state: region surfaces mapped only while their panel is open. Mapping costs a frame on
  open, and the "Qt leaves re-shown surfaces blank" problem noted in `shell.qml` applies; measure first.

**1.3 Fullscreen still composites the shell.** Every `qs-*` surface except the shadows and the
frame-space ones is on the Overlay layer, which Hyprland draws above fullscreen windows.
`contentItem.visible: !fullscreen` blanks them but they stay mapped, so every fullscreen video or game
frame still blends them (one is 4K) and direct scanout can never engage. `WlrLayershell.layer` is
writable: move them to `WlrLayer.Bottom` while `fullscreen`, then try `render.direct_scanout = 2`
(also needs hardware cursors, see 3.1). Watch for blank surfaces when they come back.

**1.4 No blur in paper style.** In paper the frame and cards are opaque, but `syncBlur`
(`quickshell/Theme.qml:58-65`) and `hypr/hyprland.lua:585-602` keep `blur = true`, so Hyprland blurs
under surfaces that cover it completely. Use `blur = level < 1` in both. The rules are written twice
(Lua and QML); a single `hypr/shell-rules.lua` that hyprland.lua `require`s and Theme.qml re-runs with
`hyprctl eval 'dofile(...)'` would keep them in sync.

**1.5 The frame and its shadow as one SDF shader** (biggest per-frame win, biggest job; iGPU only).
Each frame of a panel animation now rebuilds and re-parses an SVG path (`quickshell/Frame.qml:124-179`),
re-triangulates 2-4 `CurveRenderer` Shapes per region, and redraws the shadow as a half-res Shape layer,
a MultiEffect blur, a full-res mask layer and a mask pass (`FrameShadow.qml`, `FrameFill.qml`). One
`ShaderEffect` per surface computes the signed distance to the frame (inner rounded rect and the panel
rects, joined with a circular smooth-min of radius 20, which gives the concave joins), and from it the
gradient fill, the glass edge and the inner shadow in one pass. Animating only updates uniforms
(panel rects, join and corner radii). Compile with `/usr/lib/qt6/bin/qsb`.

**1.6 Live wallpaper.** mpvpaper damages the whole screen at the video's frame rate: the whole stack
above is recomposited and the xray blur cache rebuilt every video frame. In `hypr/mpvpaper.sh` cap the
stream (`ytdl-format=bestvideo[height<=?1440][fps<=?30]`) and add `no-audio` unless its audio is wanted
(mpvpaper plays audio by default).

## 2. Low-level (QML)

**2.1 `RectangularShadow` instead of `layer` + `MultiEffect`.** One SDF draw, no offscreen buffer, and
size, offset and blur animate for free (OPTIMIZATION.md 1a without PNGs). Up to ~200 MultiEffects exist
at runtime today. Places: `Card.qml:17`, `ListPanel.qml:111,200`, `LauncherPanel.qml:113,202`,
`PowerPanel.qml:110`, `LiveWallpaperPanel.qml:102`, `MediaPanel.qml:195,487`, `CenterSection.qml:67`,
`VScrollBar.qml:38`, and the blurred-rectangle shadows in `WallpaperPanel.qml:258`,
`WorkspacePanel.qml:96`, `CalendarPanel.qml:75`. Notable waste:
- Card's layer wraps the stats graphs: every graph repaint re-renders the card and re-blurs its shadow.
- The calendar runs two shadow effects, one of them Card's with `shadowColor: "transparent"`.
- The workspace dots' layers are reallocated every frame while their width animates.

```qml
RectangularShadow {   // in Card.qml, replacing layer.*
    anchors.fill: parent; z: -1
    radius: parent.radius; offset: Qt.vector2d(0, 3); blur: 14
    color: Qt.rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.33 * theme.opacityLevel)   // fainter under see-through (glass) cards
}
```

Values map from MultiEffect as blur ≈ `shadowBlur` x 32, offset = `shadowVerticalOffset`; tune by eye
against the "before" screenshots. Lifted rows animate `blur`/`color`/`offset` directly.

**2.2 `ScriptModel` for the filtered lists** (`ListPanel.qml:154`, `LauncherPanel.qml:154`). A plain
JS-array model resets on every keystroke and rebuilds every visible delegate (the launcher has ~60-80,
each with a MultiEffect and an icon lookup). `model: ScriptModel { values: panel.shown }` diffs.

**2.3 Wallpaper carousel** (`WallpaperPanel.qml`).
- The Repeater (line 235) makes all 90 thumbnails, each with a MultiEffect shadow, a `ClippingRectangle`
  (itself an offscreen layer + shader) and six bindings re-evaluated every frame of a slide. At most ~9
  are ever visible: keep 9 slots and map slot to file.
- Cache ~480x270 thumbnails on disk with the rounded corners baked in (OPTIMIZATION.md 1d), which
  removes the ClippingRectangle too.
- Bug: `now` (lines 104-113, run on every open at 160) spawns `wpaperctl get-all`, which right after
  login returns the boot copy `~/wallpapers/pywallpaper.jpg`; it isn't in the list, so the picker opens
  on the first wallpaper. Use `theme.wallpaper` (no process).

**2.4 The media marquee never stops** (`MediaStrip.qml:82`): it scrolls whenever a player with a long
title exists, paused or not, the live wallpaper included (it is an MPRIS player via
`/etc/mpv/scripts/mpris.so`). It is the only thing that makes the shell render every frame while idle.
Add `&& strip.media.playing` (or scroll only on hover).

**2.5 Process spawns to Quickshell APIs.**
- Keybinds: `GlobalShortcut { name: "launcher"; onPressed: ... }` in shell.qml and
  `hl.bind(mainMod .. " + R", hl.dsp.global("quickshell:launcher"))` (check the argument form in the
  stub) instead of `qs ipc call`. Keep the IpcHandlers for scripts.
- `CenterSection.qml:25-29` and `WorkspacePanel.qml:205,219-222`: `Hyprland.dispatch("hl.dsp.focus({
  workspace = " + n + " })")`; the special-workspace lookup can read
  `Hyprland.focusedMonitor.lastIpcObject` (refresh first) instead of `hyprctl monitors -j | jq`.
- `MediaPanel.qml:107-121`: `PwNodeLinkTracker { node: panel.stream }` instead of `pactl`.

## 3. Hyprland settings

- **3.1** `cursor.no_hardware_cursors = true` (`hypr/hyprland.lua:164`) is left from NVIDIA. amdgpu does
  hardware cursors; software cursors make every mouse move a composite and block direct scanout. Try
  `false`, and check the dynamic-cursors stretch mode still works.
- **3.2** `render.new_render_scheduling = true`: triple buffering when a frame runs late, meant for weak GPUs.
- **3.3** Drop `WLR_DRM_NO_ATOMIC` (line 134): aquamarine ignores `WLR_*`.
- **3.4** VRR stays off on the desktop (QD-OLED flickers with VRR); `vrr = 2` only for fullscreen games.
- **3.5** `no_anim` layer rule for `^qs-` so restarting Quickshell doesn't play the 70% pop-in.

## Testing: real input, screenshots, frame times

Everything is driven like a user would, no `qs ipc call` or other shortcuts for the thing under test:

- **Screenshots:** `grim` (full screen or `-g` region), cropped and downscaled with `magick` before being
  viewed (a full 4K frame costs a lot of tokens). Kept in a scratch directory outside the repo.
- **Keyboard:** `wtype` (real key presses through the virtual-keyboard protocol): Super+R, typing in the
  launcher, arrows, Enter, Escape.
- **Mouse:** `wlrctl pointer move/click/scroll` (virtual-pointer protocol, already installed). Its moves
  are relative and may be bent by acceleration: test accuracy first; if it drifts, place the cursor with
  `hl.dsp.cursor.move` and do the clicks, hovers and scrolls with wlrctl.
- **Smoothness:** screenshots show shape and color, not smoothness. Use `debug:overlay = true` for
  Hyprland's frame times, and short `wf-recorder` clips split into frames with `ffmpeg` to count dropped
  frames during panel opens and workspace slides. The final word is Shubham's eyes.

Safety rules while testing:
- Shubham keeps hands off the machine during a run; each run is short and announced first.
- Never click Sleep, Log out, Restart or Shut down in the power panel (opening and closing it is fine),
  never remove clipboard entries or live-wallpaper links, never test DPMS-off blindly.

Baseline ("before") set, taken once before any change:
1. Idle desktop: whole screen, plus crops of the bar, the four frame corners, the left dock.
2. Each panel open: stats, calendar, launcher (empty and with a query), clipboard, live wallpaper,
   media (if a player is up), power, workspace overview, wallpaper picker.
3. Paper and glass style for the frame and one panel; one dark/normal/light mode set.
4. `debug:overlay` readings: idle, opening the launcher, a workspace slide, a live wallpaper playing.

Each task's "after" shots repeat the relevant baseline shots at the same coordinates for side-by-side
comparison, and its functional check repeats the clicks and keys that use the changed code.
